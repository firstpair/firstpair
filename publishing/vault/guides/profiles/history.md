## Using a history and sources vault

A history vault separates the continuous narrative from its evidence graph.
Quotations should link to exact local passages or anthology headings. Claims
connect to sources, editions, translations, images, rights records, and notes
about uncertainty or dispute.

Use the Reader for the argument; use Sources, Anthology, Claims, Backlinks, and
Local graph when you want to audit it. A source note should identify the work,
witness or edition, editor or translator, locator, rights, and public reference.
Absence of attached bytes can be intentional when FirstPair may identify but
not redistribute a research copy.

Do not treat proximity in the graph as proof. Established fact, inference,
dispute, later tradition, and literary invention remain distinct editorial
states. When a bilingual alignment is incomplete, the gap should remain
visible rather than being filled by an unreviewed machine match.
