# Eigen Times Math History

By Alexy Khrabrov. Published by First Pair Press — `firstpair.press`.

A historical textbook and literature review tracing the mathematical methods behind Eigen Times, Eigen Hacks, and Anthropology, with foundational references, worked explanations, a glossary, and an index.
